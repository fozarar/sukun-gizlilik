Do I need an account?

No. Sükûn works without an account; your entries stay only on your phone.

How do I move my entries to a new phone?

On your old phone, go to Settings → My data → “Export backup” to create a file, then send it to yourself or save it to Files. On your new phone, choose that file with Settings → “Restore from backup”. Your existing entries aren’t deleted, and nothing is added twice.

My reminder doesn’t arrive.

  • Check that notifications are turned on for Sükûn in your phone’s Settings.
  • If a Focus or Do Not Disturb mode is on, the notification arrives silently.

How do I delete my entries?

Settings → My data → “Delete all data”. Removing the app also deletes everything. See the privacy policy for details.

How can I open the app quickly?

Touch and hold the Sükûn icon on your Home Screen and tap “Calm down” to go straight into a 3-minute breathing exercise.

Which languages does Sükûn support?

English and Turkish. The app follows your phone’s language. On iPhone you can also choose it in Settings → Sükûn → Language.

An important note: Sükûn is a support tool to help you cope with anxiety. It doesn’t diagnose, doesn’t provide treatment, and doesn’t replace a professional. If you’re thinking about harming yourself or someone else, or you’re in an emergency, call your local emergency number right away (for example 911 in the US, 999 in the UK, 112 in Europe).
