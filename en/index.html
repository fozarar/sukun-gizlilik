Sükûn

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Privacy policy: your data stays with you.

Sükûn doesn’t ask you to create an account and doesn’t send any data over the internet. Everything you write is stored only on your phone.

What is stored?

  • Your mood entries: rating, anxiety level, tags and notes
  • The date and length of your breathing, meditation and SOS sessions
  • Settings such as your reminder time

All of it stays on your phone, in the app’s own storage.

What doesn’t happen?

We don’t have a server; your data is never sent to us or to any other company. No advertising, analytics or tracking tools are used. Your data is never sold or shared.

Permissions

Notification permission is requested only if you turn on the daily reminder, and notifications are shown by your phone itself. The emergency call button opens your phone’s dialer; the app doesn’t record the call.

Backups

“Export backup” in Settings creates a file, and you choose where to save it or who to send it to. The file includes your notes, so keep it somewhere you trust. If your phone’s own backup (iCloud or Google) is on, the app’s data may be included in it too.

Deleting your data

“Delete all data” in Settings permanently deletes your entries and practice history. Removing the app from your phone also deletes all of its data.

Children

Sükûn isn’t designed for children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on this page, and the date at the top will be updated.

Contact

For questions or suggestions: fozarardev@gmail.com

An important note: Sükûn is a support tool to help you cope with anxiety. It doesn’t diagnose, doesn’t provide treatment, and doesn’t replace a professional. If you’re thinking about harming yourself or someone else, or you’re in an emergency, call your local emergency number right away (for example 911 in the US, 999 in the UK, 112 in Europe).
